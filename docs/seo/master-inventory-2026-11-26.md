### Honest click expectation at maturity

Expected organic clicks for the whole inventory, 6-12 months after indexing: **roughly 4,500 to 13,400 a month**, with a model midpoint near 11,300. For comparison, the 37-page gated queue alone was estimated at 900-1,600 a month.

How that is built, per page: family volume x 0.65 (variant discount) x a click-through rate by difficulty. Clear SERPs 2.5%, unknown 1.2%, authority-walled 0.3% (we expect to sit around positions 8-20 behind Mayo, Cleveland Clinic, Drugs.com and NHS even when we build through), shopping 0.6%. Gated-queue pages use the queue's own estimates.

Caveats that matter more than the midpoint:

1. **One page carries a big share of the upside.** The blood alcohol calculator (200K family, weak top-3 per receipt) models at ~3,250 clicks a month. If it lands on page 2, the total drops by about a third. The range above assumes it might only reach ~500.
2. **309 of 583 pages (53%) are expected to earn under 5 clicks a month.** They are there for topical coverage and internal linking, not traffic. Expect 0-5 clicks each.
3. **The 187 medication pages are almost all authority-walled.** They model at about 1,700 clicks a month combined (about 280 of that from the 7 gated-queue medication pages), so most individual drug pages land in the 0-20 range. They earn their place as coverage that supports the hubs (antibiotics, antidepressants, blood thinners, the master medications hub), and each needs strict safety framing.
4. **The 45 pages shipped 2026-11-24 have no performance data until the day-21 read (~2026-12-15).** If that read shows those pages getting near-zero impressions, scale this whole estimate down rather than up, and slow the build rate.
5. **Scaled-content risk is real.** Shipping hundreds of pages fast, three weeks after a trust-related ranking collapse, can hurt the whole domain. The mitigation is page quality (real mechanisms, citations, safety boxes) and a steady pace (one batch at a time, checking indexing between batches), not fewer topics.

## Ranking method

1. SERP-verified ADMITS first: the 37-page gated queue plus its 4 optional micro-tail pages, in the queue's order. They fill batch-01 and the first 16 slots of batch-02.
2. Everything else by score = family volume x monetization fit x difficulty weight. Fit: brand 1.0, ingredient 0.8, food/remedy 0.75, drink 0.65, medication 0.55, symptom 0.5, situational 0.5, science 0.4. Difficulty: clear 1.0, unknown 0.6, authority 0.35, shopping 0.25. Pages with no measured volume score as 60 a month, so they sink to the end until re-pulled.
3. Batches are filled best-first, with medication pages spread evenly (7-8 per batch) and any other class capped at 9.
